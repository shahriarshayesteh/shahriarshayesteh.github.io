Shahriar Shayesteh

PhD Candidate in Informatics · Pennsylvania State University

I am a PhD candidate in Informatics at Penn State, advised by Shomir Wilson in the Human Language Technologies Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of Responsible AI, Natural Language Processing, and Human-Centered AI. I audit LLMs and AI agents, focusing on how they select and disclose information when they act between people and other systems.

My current work studies two complementary questions: what AI agents send when acting on users' behalf, and what LLMs leave out when explaining complex information to users.

Research

AI systems increasingly sit between people and digital services, organizations, and complex information. In that position, they do more than generate language: they help determine what information passes through. I study those decisions in two directions.

When AI speaks for us, what gets sent?

AI agents increasingly communicate with third-party services on users' behalf. I study how tool interfaces shape what personal information agents can include in those requests, and when interface design creates opportunities for unnecessary disclosure.

Agentic AI · Privacy · Tool Use · Contextual Integrity
2,344 tool schemas audited · PrivateNLP @ ACL 2026

When AI explains things to us, what gets left out?

LLMs can make long consumer contracts much easier to understand, but a clear and accurate explanation can still omit consequential terms or lose important conditions and exceptions. I study what these systems emphasize, underemphasize, and leave out.

LLM Auditing · Content Selection · Legal NLP · Consumer Protection
Ongoing research · Consumer contracts

Across both directions, I use black-box auditing, controlled experiments, human annotation, and source-grounded evaluation.

Current & Recent Projects

Privacy at the Tool Interface

Studying how tool-schema design shapes the information AI agents can send to third-party services, with a focus on privacy, data minimization, and context-sensitive disclosure.

Content Selection in Consumer-Contract Explanations

Auditing whether LLM explanations preserve important topics, legally meaningful conditions, and exceptions when simplifying dense consumer contracts.

Web-Scale Privacy Measurement

Large-scale NLP and measurement research on privacy policies and web documents, including SoACer and PrivaSeer.
