Research interests

Privacy across the web and AI systems.

My work connects two worlds: what organizations say about data use, and what AI systems actually reveal when acting for people.

01

Agentic AI privacy

Privacy at the tool interface

I study the information AI agents disclose when they call tools, and how schema design can invite or prevent oversharing.

2,344 tool schemas audited · PrivateNLP @ ACL 2026

02

Web-scale measurement

Privacy norms across sectors

I build longitudinal measurements of how privacy disclosures differ across industries, services, and time.

59,590 matched policies · Longitudinal study

03

NLP systems & datasets

Context-aware web classification

I develop datasets and language-model pipelines that recover service context from noisy, heterogeneous websites.

195,495 websites · ACM DocEng 2025
