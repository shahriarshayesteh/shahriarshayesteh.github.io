I am a PhD candidate in Informatics at Penn State, advised by Shomir Wilson in the Human Language Technologies Lab.

My research focuses on two complementary areas: agentic AI privacy—how LLMs, tools, and context shape privacy risks when agentic systems act on users’ behalf—and NLP for consumer-oriented legal documents—how language technologies can help consumers navigate contracts and policies, and the failure modes that can limit their usefulness.

Research

AI systems increasingly mediate information between people, digital services, and complex documents. I study this role through two NLP-centered research areas: privacy in agentic AI systems and language technologies for consumer-oriented legal documents.

Agentic AI Privacy

Agentic AI systems combine LLMs, tools, context, and system orchestration to act on users' behalf. I study how these components shape personal-information disclosure, data minimization, and privacy risks when agents communicate with third-party services.

Tool-Using LLMs · Data Minimization · Contextual Integrity · Information Disclosure
Diagnostic audit of 2,344 tool schemas · Controlled behavioral evaluations ongoing

NLP for Consumer-Oriented Legal Documents

  • COLDs

    Consumer-oriented legal documents—such as contracts, privacy policies, terms of service, and health care agreements—are often difficult to navigate and use. I study how NLP and LLM systems can help consumers find, compare, and understand consequential information in these documents, as well as the failure modes that can make those tools incomplete or misleading.

    Natural Language Processing · Large Language Models · Legal NLP · Consumer Empowerment
    Contracts · Privacy Policies · Terms of Service · Health Care Agreements

  • PrivaSeer

    PrivaSeer is a large-scale search engine and research corpus designed to make website privacy policies transparent, searchable, and discoverable at web scale. [1] [2] [3]

    I am a member of the PrivaSeer project team. My work contributes to sector classification and longitudinal measurement of disclosure patterns across online services.

    Privacy Policies · Web-Scale NLP · Corpus Infrastructure · Privacy Measurement
